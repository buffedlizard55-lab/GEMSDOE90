## 2. Frozen Three-Experiment Ablation Plan (Budget `<= 3` Experiments)

All three experiments share the exact same **spatially-blocked hide-and-recover segment evaluator** (`vendor/gems57/evaluate_holdout.py`, `VERSION = "gems57-pooled-hide-v2"`, `seeds=(20, 21)`, `modes=("all", "detached")`, quadrant erosion `12 px = 1.2 km`, pixel-exact visible-fault mask, `alpha = 0.2, beta = 0.8, R = 300 m`, 1,000-draw paired 20 km physical spatial-block percentile 95% CI). Zero hyperparameters are tuned after inspecting holdout scores.

1. **Experiment 1 (`exp1_unsupervised_mpst_submod38k`) — Zero-Label Physical Operator Test**:
   - Deterministic rank-normalized synthesis of three label-free physical indices: (a) 8-azimuth cross-strike odd-step potential-field index (`rtp`, `iso_grav_anom`, `tmi_hg`, `iso_grav_anom_hg`), (b) shallow hydrothermal demagnetization + potassic/MT conduit index (`TMI_up150 - rtp`, `-tmi_vg`, `K/ThK`, `cond_surf`, GDR 1391 geothermometers), and (c) 3DEP 1 m LiDAR + 100 m topographic asymmetric scarp index, multiplied by a two-domain corroboration gate and allocated via exact 25-offset `R = 300 m` CELF submodular dot placement (`38,000` unit binary dots on `d_vis > 2.0 px`).
2. **Experiment 2 (`exp2_nested_loqo_blind_worm_submod38k`) — Catalogue-Free Nested LOQO Two-Stage Multi-Physics + Steerable Worm Ensemble**:
   - Stage-1 `HistGradientBoostingClassifier` (`max_iter=140, max_leaf_nodes=31, min_samples_leaf=80, learning_rate=0.06, l2_regularization=2.0`) trained strictly out-of-quadrant (`quad != q`, eroded `12 px`) on `36` localized static multi-physics features (**zero** catalogue-distance features), with `C(4, 2) = 6` nested pairwise-quadrant models supplying strictly out-of-fold Stage-1 surfaces (`p_joint`, `p_potfield`, `p_morpho`, `p_hydrostrain`) to Stage-2.
   - Stage-2 `HistGradientBoostingClassifier` (`max_iter=110, max_leaf_nodes=23, min_samples_leaf=100, learning_rate=0.05, l2_regularization=3.0`) over `18` 2D multi-scale Difference-of-Gaussians, `800 m` & `1,800 m` 8-azimuth steerable worm line-integrals/anisotropies, `second_best_domain` corroboration, and concealment-depth (`depth_to_base_surf`, `lidar_valid`) interactions; isotonic-calibrated on inner folds and emitted via exact `R = 300 m` CELF submodular dot placement (`38,000` unit binary dots on `d_vis > 2.0 px`).
3. **Experiment 3 (`exp3_dual_coulomb_mpst_submod38k`) — Full `H90-1` Synthesis (Concealment-Conditioned Multi-Physics Worm Surface + Strictly Fold-Visible Coulomb Fault-Tip Cone & En-Echelon Relay Transfer)**:
   - Combines `Exp 2`'s strictly out-of-quadrant Stage-2 probability surface with a strictly fold-visible (`ctx.visible(key)` in holdout; `existing_faults.tif` only at full-grid export) **Coulomb Tip-Extension & Relay-Step Structural Transfer Model** (`3` features: capped visible fault distance `d_vis in (2.0, 35.0] px`, directional forward-tip wing-crack lobe `cos^4(theta_tip) * exp(-r/10 px)` ahead of visible fault endpoints, and en-echelon inter-tip relay bridge potential), calibrated out-of-fold and emitted via exact `R = 300 m` CELF submodular expected-credit allocation (`38,000` unit binary dots on `d_vis > 2.0 px`).
