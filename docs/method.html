GEMSDOE90

DOE GEMS Prize (DrivenData #306) — credit-efficient hidden-fault discovery, auditable end to end.

SubmissionHow to submit Method & evidenceLane H90 Repository

Hypothesis

The DOE GEMS metric is won on credit efficiency, not on map accuracy. Because FN_w = |G| - TP_w, the index collapses to T / (0.2T + 0.2(S-M) + 0.8|G|), so each unit-mass dot that is far from truth costs a fixed 0.2 in the denominator and pays only its expected coverage credit. A fold-honest discovery model that ranks pixels by the probability of hosting an unmapped fault trace, emitted greedily until the marginal credit falls below the break-even 0.2*theta/(1-0.2*theta), should therefore beat a fixed-radius collar placed around the existing catalogue at the same budget.

Mechanism

In the Great Basin >85% of geothermal systems sit in fault interaction zones - step-overs and relay ramps (~32-40%), horsetail terminations (~16-25%), fault intersections (~22-26%) and accommodation zones (~6-9%) - and only ~2% on main range-front segments (Faulds & Hinz 2015; Faulds et al. 2021a, 2026). Unmapped traces are therefore expected as linking strands, splays and cross-faults near but off mapped master traces, where potential-field gradients (magnetic terracing, gravity horizontal gradient over basin-floor steps), radiometric K-enrichment from up-flow alteration, lidar scarp morphometry and geodetic strain all respond. The learner combines 54 such static features with 9 catalogue-geometry features rebuilt per fold.

Named non-fault process that could mimic it

Quaternary alluvial-fan toe scarps and shoreline terraces of pluvial Lake Lahontan. Both produce metre-scale, kilometres-long linear breaks in slope with a lidar signature essentially identical to a young fault scarp, and the Lahontan shorelines additionally follow elevation contours around every range front in the scene. They carry no magnetic or gravity offset, which is why the stack keeps potential-field gradients and the scarp morphometry as separate channels rather than fusing them.

Pipeline

  1. scripts/restore_data.py — restore and SHA-256 verify all 26 pinned inputs into .cache/gems_data/ (never committed).
  2. scripts/run_discovery.py features — 63 leak-free static features (GeoDAWN magnetics/gravity/strain/conductivity, 3DEP-derived lidar scarp morphometry, radiometrics and ratios, SGMC geologic-map faults, GDR 1391 thermal wells/springs, Quaternary slip-rate density, volcanic vents) plus 9 catalogue features rebuilt per fold.
  3. scripts/run_discovery.py folds — buffered whole-segment hide-and-recover; a gradient boosted model per fold trained only on other folds' withheld segments.
  4. scripts/run_discovery.py deploy — the same learner with the whole catalogue visible, giving the hidden-fault probability field.
  5. scripts/invert_blind_probes.py — |G| from the blind lattice probe.
  6. scripts/calibrate.py — fit the field against 14 owner-reported scores.
  7. scripts/make_submission.py — greedy DTI-optimal emission, budget at the maximum of the exact predicted-DTI curve, lane screen, format gate, GeoTIFF.
  8. scripts/evaluate_holdout.py — pooled HOLDOUT-DTI with block-bootstrap CIs.

Known irregularities

  • Every historical score used for calibration is OWNER-REPORTED; no submission-page receipt is in this repository. The calibration is therefore conditional on that list being accurate.
  • labels.tif and existing_faults.tif in the pinned mirror are byte-identical (SHA-256 7ba308cc…), i.e. the training labels are the catalogue.
  • Two registry rasters (hedge_v2, ens12) are pixel-identical off-catalogue and differ by 54,533 on-catalogue pixels, and both are reported at 0.1563 — direct evidence that catalogued fault pixels are masked out of scoring.
  • The kernel's distance is assumed Euclidean in pixels; the published definition gives k(d)=max(1-d/300 m, 0) without naming the metric.
  • The prompt quotes three different "current leaderboard" values (0.4413, 0.3195, 0.3049 in an older sibling repository). None is verifiable from this sandbox; the live leaderboard must be read by a human on the day.
